Course A · Kanda, Sugamo and Daikanyama

Bows, ramen & wrestling

The bow shop, then a famous spicy ramen, then two floors of pro wrestling goods in Sugamo. It is the day after Ryogoku, so you will have things to look for.

The day

  1. 10:00
    Your hotel lobbyI pick you up. Kanda is about 10 minutes away.
  2. 10:15
    Koyama Kyugu, Kanda main storeBow maker for over 200 years, with its own workshop for bows, arrows and gloves. You can hold the bows and ask the staff anything. This is the main stop, so we take our time.
  3. 11:20
    Early lunch at KikanboSpicy miso ramen with chili and Sichuan pepper. We go before 12:00 to skip the line.
  4. 12:15
    Train to SugamoAbout 20 minutes on the Yamanote line.
  5. 12:40
    Toudoukan pro wrestling shopTwo floors of wrestling and MMA goods: masks, signed boards, old programs, DVDs and figures. One minute from the station.
  6. 13:45
    Train to DaikanyamaAbout 40 minutes.
  7. 14:30
    Daikanyama: Tsutaya Books and Log RoadQuiet streets, a big design bookshop and a garden path on an old rail line.
  8. 15:30
    DoneAt Daikanyama station, or I ride back with you.

The route

Course B · Old temple, river boat, garden

Asakusa, and the river home

You will have been awake since a quarter past four and had lunch already. This course is built so that the moving after that is done sitting down: a temple in the morning district, then a boat that carries you back down the Sumida and drops you almost at your door. Nothing here needs booking and nothing here is far.

The day

  1. 13:15
    After lunchAbout 25 minutes by train from Ginza to Asakusa. You can sleep on it.
  2. 13:45
    Senso-ji, AsakusaTokyo’s oldest temple, founded in 628. You come in under a five-metre paper lantern, down a street of stalls selling rice crackers and fans that has been a shopping street for three hundred years. Flat the whole way, benches in the courtyard.
  3. 15:00
    Something sweet on NakamiseMelon bread straight out of the oven, or a bag of hot senbei. We eat as we walk back to the pier.
  4. 15:40
    The boat down the SumidaForty minutes on the water. Twelve bridges, each one a different colour, the Skytree behind you and the city sliding past. You are sitting the whole way and there is a toilet on board.
  5. 16:30
    Hama-rikyu GardensThe boat lands inside a 400-year-old shogun’s garden with saltwater ponds. Flat gravel paths, benches all the way round, the skyline standing over the pines.
  6. 17:15
    Tea over the pond, if you want itA tea house on stilts out over the water. Matcha whisked in front of you with a seasonal sweet. Entirely optional.
  7. 18:00
    Back at the hotelFifteen minutes on foot, or one short taxi.

The route
